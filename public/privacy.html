Overview

Vervox is a privacy-first, offline-capable task manager built for single-user focus and zero-password collaboration with one partner or a small Group Pod. This document explains — in plain language — what data Vervox touches, where it lives, who can see it, and how you stay in control. It applies to the Vervox Progressive Web App (the “App”) and the related websites operated by FabuyoDevs.

Our guiding principle is data minimisation: Vervox asks for the least amount of information required to make the product work, keeps it for the shortest practical period, and never sells it.

1.Application Architecture & Data Minimisation

Vervox operates as a Progressive Web App (PWA). It can be installed to a home screen or dock and runs offline, syncing whenever a connection is available.

  • Firebase Anonymous Authentication. When cloud sync is enabled, Vervox signs your device in anonymously. This produces an opaque technical identifier used only to authorise database reads and writes.
  • Device-bound UUIDs. On first launch, Vervox generates a cryptographically random device UUID (using the browser’s crypto.randomUUID() where available) and stores it locally in IndexedDB. This UUID — not your name, email or phone number — is how a paired device or pod member recognises you.
  • No traditional accounts. You are not required to create an account with an email address, password, phone number, or any personal identity credential to use Vervox’s core task-management features — including tasks, subtasks, schedules, alarms, reminders, and 1-on-1 pairing.
  • No analytics or ad trackers. Vervox does not embed third-party advertising SDKs, cross-site analytics beacons, fingerprinting scripts, or behavioural profiling tools.
  • Optional display name. If you choose a display name for a Group Pod, it is stored alongside your device UUID and shown only to pod members. You may leave it blank or change it at any time.

If you never enable cloud sync, Vervox runs entirely on a local, on-device engine. In that mode none of your task content leaves your device.

2.Task Data & Partner Synchronisation

When cloud sync is enabled, task content — titles, completion statuses, timestamps, subtasks (sub-items), priority, schedule and alarm settings, and optional location tags — is synchronised using Google Cloud Firestore so that changes appear in real time on every authorised device.

  • Row-Level Security (RLS). Access control is strictly enforced server-side by Firestore security rules. A task document records the exact list of device UUIDs and authentication UIDs permitted to see it. The database refuses any read, update, or delete request from a caller that is not on that list — the app’s interface is never the only gatekeeper.
  • 1-on-1 pairing. Two devices link using a single-use, six-character invitation code (for example TASK-8F2A) that expires after 15 minutes and is destroyed the instant it is redeemed. Attempts are rate-limited to protect against brute-force guessing.
  • Group Pods (up to 6 members). Pod invitations use the same single-use, expiring code mechanism (prefixed POD-). A pod admits a maximum of six device UUIDs, and Vervox refuses to widen a task’s audience beyond that limit. Tasks shared in a pod are readable only by current pod members.
  • Unlinking. Unpairing or leaving a pod revokes future access immediately. You should also archive or delete any tasks you no longer wish to share.

Vervox does not use your task content for advertising, model training, or profiling, and does not disclose it to third parties except as described in this policy.

3.Third-Party Media Attachments

Vervox can attach proof-of-performance media to a completed task — for example a photo snapshot or a short (maximum 15-second) voice note.

  • Media does not sit on Vervox core database servers. Media payloads are not stored as binary content inside the Vervox task database.
  • External link conversion. Media assets are converted to external string URLs through third-party link converter / image hosting APIs (for example ImgBB or FreeImageHost). Only the resulting secure HTTPS link is referenced on the task document.
  • Third-party terms apply. Because the file lives with the hosting provider, that provider’s own terms and retention practices govern the stored asset. Anyone who holds the direct URL may be able to open it, so please avoid attaching sensitive documents, identity cards, or confidential material.
  • Your camera and microphone. Media capture happens only when you explicitly tap the capture control. Camera and microphone permissions are granted and revocable through your browser or operating system; declining them simply disables attachments.
  • Removal. Deleting an attachment removes the reference from the task. Removal of the underlying hosted file is subject to the hosting provider’s deletion process.

4.Payment Processing & Multi-Currency Billing (Flutterwave)

Vervox offers optional paid upgrades: a Themes & Sounds Pack, a Cloud Proof & Media subscription, a lifetime Group Pods unlock, and the Pro Bundle.

  • Processed externally by Flutterwave. All digital microtransactions, lifetime pod unlocks, and Pro tier upgrades are processed externally through Flutterwave’s hosted checkout. Payment credentials are entered on Flutterwave-controlled surfaces.
  • We never touch your financial data. Vervox never collects, processes, transmits, or stores credit or debit card numbers, bank account details, card PINs, CVV/CVC codes, OTPs, or any other sensitive financial credential on its own servers or databases.
  • What we keep. We retain only a minimal purchase record — which plan was bought, when, and a locally generated transaction reference — so that your entitlement tier can be restored on your device and, where cloud sync is enabled, associated with your anonymous account.
  • Multi-currency pricing. Vervox displays prices in NGN, USD, KES, GBP and EUR (and other supported currencies) using a static pricing table. Currency selection relies on lightweight IP-based country detection performed at app start. This is a one-shot lookup used purely to choose a display and billing currency.
  • No continuous location tracking. Vervox does not store GPS history and does not track your movements. Precise location is requested only if you voluntarily tap “Tag location” on an individual task, and that coordinate is saved on that task alone and shared only with your paired device or pod.
  • Refunds & chargebacks. Refunds, disputes, and chargebacks are handled under Flutterwave’s and the applicable payment provider’s policies. Vervox can revoke or restore an entitlement to match a verified refund outcome.

5.PWA Local Storage, Service Workers & Notifications

To make Vervox fast and usable without a connection, the App stores data on your device:

  • IndexedDB. Holds your task cache for offline reading, your locally generated device UUID, archived completions, and any media blobs when you are using the on-device (server-less) engine.
  • LocalStorage. Stores lightweight preferences — theme, chime pack, quiet-hours window, notification switch, purchase receipts, and dismissal flags for banners such as the install prompt.
  • Service Worker (sw.js). Caches app assets for instant, offline-first loading, supports background push delivery, and performs automatic background synchronisation of pending task changes once your network reconnects — so edits made offline are queued and flushed rather than lost.
  • Notifications & chimes are optional. Web Push and local notifications (scheduled alarms, partner completion updates, nudges) are delivered only after you grant the operating-system level permission, and can be turned off at any time from Settings → Preferences or revoked in your browser’s site settings. Completion chimes are synthesised locally in your browser and can be muted with the Audio Chimes toggle or silenced with Quiet Hours.
  • Push subscriptions. If you enable push, your browser issues a push subscription endpoint. It is used solely to deliver the notifications described above.

Local data never leaves your device except as described in Sections 2–4, and is removed when you clear site data or uninstall the App.

6.Your Rights & Data Clearing

Because Vervox is accountless, you remain in direct control of your data at all times. You may:

  • Erase local data. Clear the App’s IndexedDB and LocalStorage from your browser’s site settings (or by uninstalling the installed PWA). This deletes the offline task cache, device UUID, archived completions, and local purchase receipts.
  • Reset pairing. Unlink a partner or leave a Group Pod at any time from the pairing and pod screens. This immediately revokes the other party’s future access.
  • Delete tasks. Delete individual tasks, archive completed ones, or clear every task on the device from Settings → Clear / Archive Tasks.
  • Revoke permissions. Withdraw camera, microphone, geolocation, or notification permissions from your operating system at any time; Vervox degrades gracefully when they are denied.
  • Request assistance. Contact FabuyoDevs (Section 8) for help removing data from shared spaces or for any privacy question.

A note on shared data: if a task was shared with a partner or pod, that party may have already synchronised a copy to their own device. Unlinking stops future sharing; to remove content completely, delete the task before unlinking.
